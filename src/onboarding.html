🎯 Smart Blocking

Automatically blocks TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and other addictive short-form content. Customize which sites to block in settings.

🧠 Mindful Challenges

To take a break, complete a mindful moment — a breathing exercise, a math problem, or a brain teaser. This moment of friction helps you think twice.

⏱️ Timed Breaks

After completing a challenge, you get 2, 5, or 10 minutes of access. Then you're back to focus mode.

ℹ️ Focus mode means the extension will block distracting content again, helping you stay productive.

🛡️ Smart Limits

After 2 consecutive breaks, you'll have a 15-minute cooldown before taking another.

ℹ️ This pause helps break the habit loop—research shows short delays reduce impulsive behavior and help you regain control.
